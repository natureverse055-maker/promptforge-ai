About the project

Make your video ideas easier to explain.

PromptForge AI is an independent prompt-writing tool designed to help creators turn a short idea into a clearer text-to-video prompt. The tool organizes an idea into format, timeline, camera, audio, quality, ending, and negative-prompt sections.

Who is it for?

It is built for beginners, short-form video creators, educators, marketers, and anyone experimenting with AI video tools.

How it works

The current starter version runs in your browser and assembles a prompt from the settings you choose. It does not call an AI model, and it does not guarantee that another video generator will produce a particular result.

Independent project

PromptForge AI is an independent tool and is not affiliated with Google, OpenAI, or any third-party video generator. Product names belong to their respective owners.
