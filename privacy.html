Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This starter website generates prompts locally in your browser. In its current version, the text you enter is not intentionally sent to a PromptForge AI server, and the website does not create an account or store your prompt history.

Hosting and technical logs

The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, and request logs to deliver and protect the website. Review the hosting provider's current privacy documentation for details.

Cookies and advertising

This starter does not currently include live advertising code. If advertising services such as Google AdSense are added later, those services may use cookies or similar technologies as described in their own policies. Update this page before enabling ads and provide any consent or disclosures required in the countries you target.

Third-party services

If external analytics, AI APIs, sign-in, or payment providers are added in a future version, this policy must be updated to explain what data is shared and why.

Children and contact

This tool is intended for a general audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children. For privacy questions, use the contact instructions on our Contact page.
